Your Irish & trad music collection,
in your pocket

Organise your tunes into collections and take quizzes to help you learn.

With MyTunes you can:
  • Create collections of tunes. For example: To practise, Favourites, Local Session.
  • Add tunes and find their reference on TheSession.org.
  • Quiz yourself on a collection to help you learn the tunes.
  • Share collections of tunes.
  • Personalise tune information such as the key or your favourite recording.
  • Create sets and set lists.
  • Share set lists or export them to PDF.

Tune data sourced from TheSession.org: the world's largest community for Irish traditional music.

Also:
  • No ads.
  • No account needed.
  • You own your data.

Everything you need for a session

Tune library

Browse and organise your jigs, reels, hornpipes, and more, sourced directly from TheSession.org.

Audio playback

Hear the opening of any tune, played with synthesised audio generated from its ABC notation.

Quiz mode

An adaptive quiz that rewards getting it right first time with gold star animations.

Dark mode

Full light and dark theme support, easy on the eyes at late-night sessions.

Built for the session

MyTunes is an iOS and Android app for traditional Irish and folk musicians who want an offline-ready tune companion to bring to every session.

Important tune data (settings, ABC notation) comes from TheSession.org, the open community archive that collects Irish traditional music.

You can also add your own notes, favourite recordings, and more.
