Built for the session

MyTunes is an iOS and Android app for traditional Irish and folk musicians who want a beautiful, offline-ready tune companion to bring to every session.

All tune data — settings, ABC notation, recordings, and aliases — comes from thesession.org, the open community archive that has been collecting and curating Irish traditional music since 1999.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the tune data come from?
All tunes, settings, and ABC notation are sourced from thesession.org, the world's largest open archive of Irish traditional music, maintained by a global community of musicians since 1999.
Does MyTunes work offline?
Yes. Once your collection is downloaded, the core app works offline. Only sheet music, audio playback, and audio quiz need an internet connection. Your collections keep available during sessions in places with no signal.
Which instruments is it designed for?
MyTunes is instrument-agnostic: any musician can use it.
Is the app free?
The core app is free to download and use. An optional Supporter Badge is available as an in-app purchase for those who want to support the development of the app.
How does the quiz mode work?
The quiz plays the first notes of a tune and asks you to identify it by name. Answer correctly on the first attempt and you earn a gold star. There also other kinds of quiz that help learning.
Which platforms are supported?
MyTunes is available on iOS (iPhone and iPad) and Android. Both versions are built from the same codebase and receive updates simultaneously.
