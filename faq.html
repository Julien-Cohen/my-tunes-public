Frequently asked questions

Where does the tune data come from?
All settings and ABC notation are sourced from TheSession.org.
Does MyTunes work offline?
Yes. Once your collection is built, the core app works offline. Your collections stay available during sessions in places with no signal.
Which instruments is it designed for?
MyTunes is instrument-agnostic: any musician can use it.
Is the app free?
The core app is free to download and use. An optional Pro Badge is available as an in-app purchase. This unlocks unlimited tune cards, collections and setlists.
How does the quiz mode work?
The quiz plays the first notes of a tune and asks you to identify it by name. Answer correctly on the first attempt and you earn a star. There are also other kinds of quiz that help you learn.
Which platforms are supported?
MyTunes is available on iOS (iPhone and iPad) and Android. Both versions are built from the same codebase and receive updates simultaneously.
